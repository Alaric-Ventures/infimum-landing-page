About Infimum Consulting

The business comes first. The technology follows.

Infimum Consulting is an IT consultancy and managed services partner. We work with organisations to plan, design and deliver technology across custom applications, digital experiences, cloud infrastructure and Microsoft 365.

Our starting point is the business need: how work gets done, where time is lost and what your teams and customers actually require. From there, we shape a solution that fits — then stay involved as it is implemented, adopted and refined.

Our mission is straightforward: high-quality technology services at a sensible cost, with engagement options that respect each client’s needs and budget.
